processing power and finally current systems are susceptible to various forms of spoofing known as adversarial ai we are working to address these challenges and vulnerabilities through multiple efforts most of which will lever the complementary roles of the joint artificial intelligence center the jaic and the usdre enterprise the jaic will offer a means to rapidly determine the appropriate
